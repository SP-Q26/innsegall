Agent piping (CLI JSON)

On the operator Mac after install:

innsegall check --json --smoke --force

Prints a validated Battle Scout card to stdout · no HTML · use in scripts or local automation. Production scouts respect Voyage quota unless --force (operator/dev).

The installer clones Innsegall, runs innsegall bootstrap (welcome Battle Scout + launchd schedule), and prints your next Voyage date.

Two steps to protect your work

Optional · Innsegall does not back up your Mac. After a scare, a local backup lane helps you recover if files are lost or encrypted.

  1. Set up Time Machine. Apple’s built-in Time Machine is your best defense against malware or accidental deletion. Plug in an external hard drive, go to System Settings → General → Time Machine, and turn it on. It runs quietly in the background and saves everything automatically.
  2. Use a secure cloud backup. If your external drive is lost or damaged, a cloud backup keeps you safe. Services like iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or Microsoft OneDrive can automatically sync your active work folders.

Terminal · innsegall backup · prints these steps and opens this section on your Mac.
