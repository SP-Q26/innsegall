Battle Scout · arm any AI

Goal: weaponize your receipt with any AI assistant from anywhere you already work · not a new subscription · not a cloud upload from Innsegall.

  1. Send the scout · innsegall run or innsegall voyage
  2. Open the Battle Scout HTML in your browser
  3. Tap Copy Battle Scout for AI in the footer
  4. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, or DeepSeek · phone or desktop

The paste block uses innsegall-battle-scout-ai/v1 · full audit log you chose to share. Compact machine context also lives in #innsegall-scout-data on the same page. See Privacy · what we do not receive.
