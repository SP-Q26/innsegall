After a scare popup, many operators reach for their iPhone first · search · chat · call a friend. Innsegall’s beachhead is still the Macintosh · read-only scout · Battle Scout receipt. i0 names the honest next step: an iOS app that remembers and explains what the Mac scout already said · not a pocket antivirus.
