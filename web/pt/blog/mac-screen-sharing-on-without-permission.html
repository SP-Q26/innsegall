Security researchers and national agencies have repeatedly warned that internet-exposed Screen Sharing on macOS is a real attack path · not a browser popup hoax. Finding the toggle on can feel like someone is already inside your Mac · most of the time you need settings truth first.

If you are helping someone (human or AI)

  1. Name the surface. Browser locker vs real System Settings · fix the trap before “whole Mac infected” talk.
  2. Evidence before subscriptions. No cleaner upsell from the same scammer who caused the scare.
  3. Macintosh scout last. Enviar o scout · read-only Battle Scout · paste for any AI assistant.

Check what you actually enabled

System Settings → General → Sharing · note whether Screen Sharing or Remote Management is on. If you use it for work, leave it · but restrict who can connect and turn it off when you do not need it.

Why 5900 shows up in the news

Screen Sharing listens on the network when sharing is enabled. Advisories in 2025–2026 focused on Macs reachable from the public internet · often with weak or missing authentication. Patch via Software Update · do not pay a random “support” number from a popup.
