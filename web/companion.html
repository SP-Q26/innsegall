Install on iPhone or iPad

Takes about two minutes · data stays on this device · not antivirus.

  1. Step 1 · Open Companion in Safari Type or tap innsegall.com/companion · use Safari (Chrome on iOS cannot Add to Home Screen the same way).
  2. Step 2 · Add to Home Screen Tap the Share button (square with up arrow) · scroll · Add to Home Screen · name it Innsegall · Add.
  3. Step 3 · Open from the icon Launch from your Home Screen · full-screen inbox · import paste from your Mac scout · tap Load sample to see how it looks.
  4. Step 4 · After a Mac scout On the Mac Battle Scout · tap Copy for AI · paste into Companion · or share the JSON to Files and use Files import.

No App Store app today · Innsegall does not scan your phone or tablet · the Mac runs the scout · this install is Companion on Home Screen plus a pinned panic guide.
