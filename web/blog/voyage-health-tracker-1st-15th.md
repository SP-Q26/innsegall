## What Voyage is (and is not)

**Voyage is:**

- A **scheduled reminder** to run a **Battle Scout** hygiene check.
- A **local history chart** · verdict and category trends over time.
- A **rhythm** that prevents small drifts (new login item, odd extension) from becoming year-long fog.

**Voyage is not:**

- Cloud surveillance of your files.
- A subscription that holds your Mac hostage.
- A leaderboard or shame score.
- Real-time antivirus scanning duplicating Apple’s work.

Data stays on your Mac until **you** share a card. The chart is built from **your saved scouts** · not from telemetry we sell.

## Why the 1st and 15th?

Two anchors per month match how real humans live:

- **Paycheck rhythm** · many households touch finances around the 1st and mid-month.
- **Low enough** to avoid alert fatigue · not daily nagging.
- **High enough** to catch drift before it stacks · a login agent planted on the 3rd is caught by the 15th.

Think dental cleaning, not DEFCON 1. The tired Norseman does not swing the axe at every shadow · he sends a **scout** on a schedule and reads the runes when the scout returns.

| Date | Voyage nudge | Typical action |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| **1st** | “Chart point due” | `innsegall voyage` or open the app prompt |
| **15th** | Mid-month check | Same · compare to last point |

Miss a date? No punishment. Run when you can · the chart simply has a gap, not a red shame badge.

## What gets tracked on the chart

Each Voyage point comes from a completed **Battle Scout** · usually the **Is my Macintosh okay?** hygiene flow. The chart summarizes:

- **Verdict** over time · clear, fix list, escalate.
- **Category flags** · launch items, profiles, DNS, browsers · counts and trends, not filenames.
- **Engine version** · so you know which scout generation produced the point.

You are looking for **patterns**, not perfection:

- Three months of **clear** · keep sailing.
- New **fix list** on login items after you installed “free wallpaper” software · remove the item; next point should calm.
- Sudden **escalate** · **Sound the Horn**, share the card, bring your clan.

This is **Read the runes** at voyage scale · macro clarity, not micro panic.
