## What Apple already gives you

macOS is not Windows circa 2003. Built-in layers matter:

- **Gatekeeper** blocks unsigned or unnotarized software unless you explicitly override.
- **XProtect** scans known malware signatures on download and launch.
- **Sandboxing** limits what App Store and notarized apps can touch.
- **System Integrity Protection (SIP)** guards system files from casual tampering.
- **Signed updates** through Software Update · the single most important hygiene act.

None of this is perfect. Determined attackers and **you clicking Open** can still win. But the baseline is stronger than fear merchants admit · because fear is their product.

## Why “install antivirus” is often the wrong reflex

Traditional antivirus on Mac tends to:

- **Slow the machine** with always-on scanning duplicating Apple’s work.
- **Cry wolf** with “potentially unwanted” flags that train you to ignore alerts.
- **Sell upgrades** with the same urgency aesthetics as fake virus popups.
- **Ask for broad access** · sometimes more than the malware you fear.

That is not say every security tool is evil. Developers, researchers, and high-risk roles may want layered defenses. For the tired parent, the freelance designer, the retiree with Gmail · **hygiene beats bloat.**

## What hygiene actually means on a Macintosh

Hygiene is **infrastructure housekeeping** · not personality judgment. Think dock lines, not battle axes.

| Area | Why it matters |
|------|----------------|
| **Login items & LaunchAgents** | Persistence · where adware survives reboots |
| **Configuration profiles** | VPN/work legit · or sideloaded control |
| **DNS & proxy settings** | Hijacked resolvers redirect your banking |
| **Browser extensions** | Hidden search hijacks and credential skimmers |
| **Sharing services** | Screen Sharing exposed to the internet is a real CVE class |
| **Recent installs** | Especially DMGs from DMs and “free codecs” |
| **Hosts file & Gatekeeper status** | Quick signals something tampered with routing or trust |

A good hygiene pass does not scream **INFECTED**. It says: *launch item “upd_helper” · you did not install this · here is how to remove it.*

That is the **laser dirk** · precision, not rage.
