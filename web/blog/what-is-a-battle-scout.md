## What is inside a Battle Scout card

A Battle Scout is an **HTML card** (and JSON under the hood) generated on your Mac. Typical sections:

### Verdict

One of three plain outcomes:

- **Clear** · path marked; nothing urgent.
- **Fix list** · small surgical steps · disable one login item, remove one extension.
- **Escalate** · **Sound the Horn** · passwords may be involved; bring your clan or a Beacon.

### Flow label

Which question you asked:

- **Is my Macintosh okay?** · general hygiene.
- **I clicked a suspicious link** · post-click triage.
- **Is my project safe?** · repo/supply-chain bounded check.

### Stats

Pass · warn · fail counts across categories. Not a malware score · a **receipt of what was looked at**.

### Checks

Named categories · `launch_ghosts`, `dns_resolvers`, `config_profiles`, browser profiles, Gatekeeper, and more. Each line is **pass**, **warn**, or **fail** with human-readable detail on the card.

### Fixes

Ordered steps when the verdict is fix list or escalate. Low-risk first. No “buy premium to remove.”

### Parley (optional)

Plain-English summary · static from the card, or live **Solas** if you add an API key. Bounded help, not a service siege.

### Voyage hook

If you run scouts on a schedule, the card links to your **Voyage chart** · health over time on the 1st and 15th.

## Battle Scouts vs antivirus reports

Antivirus often optimizes for **engagement**: red banners, daily scans, upgrade prompts.

Battle Scouts optimize for **closure**:

- **Name what was checked.**
- **Name what is gone.**
- **Mark the path clear.**

If the scout says clear, you are allowed to close the laptop and sleep. If it says fix list, you have a todo list · not a trauma response. If it says escalate, **Sound the Horn** · evidence on the table.
