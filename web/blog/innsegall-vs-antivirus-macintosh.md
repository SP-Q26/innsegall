# Innsegall vs Antivirus on Macintosh

**Different jobs.** Antivirus watches the gate forever. Innsegall answers **after** the scare — with a **Battle Scout** receipt.

## What antivirus does

Malwarebytes, Bitdefender, Norton: real-time signatures, web shields, scheduled scans. Valuable for always-on protection.

## What Innsegall does

- Post-popup triage: fake virus alerts, "call Apple" scams
- After a suspicious link: DNS, launch items, browsers, recent installs
- **Voyage** rhythm: 1st & 15th hygiene chart — calm, not nagware
- Plain-language **LIKELY_OK / FIX_LIST / ESCALATE** — no fear UI

## When to use both

1. Keep your AV if you want continuous scanning.
2. Run Innsegall when panic hits — or on Voyage days for drift.
3. Share the Battle Scout with family IT instead of a screenshot spiral.
