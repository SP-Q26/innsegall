What antivirus does

Malwarebytes, Bitdefender, Norton: real-time signatures, web shields, scheduled scans. Valuable for always-on protection.

What Innsegall does

  • Post-popup triage: fake virus alerts, "call Apple" scams

When to use both

  1. Keep your AV if you want continuous scanning.
