When a website fills the screen, plays an alarm, and lists a toll-free number, that is the attack · not evidence that XProtect failed. Your Mac is usually fine once the tab is gone.

If you are helping someone (human or AI)

  1. Name the surface. Browser locker vs real System Settings · fix the trap before “whole Mac infected” talk.
  2. Evidence before subscriptions. No cleaner upsell from the same scammer who caused the scare.

Escape without paying

Force Quit the browser (Apple menu → Force Quit). On Safari, hold Shift when reopening so the trap tab does not restore · Shift-reopen guide. Chrome: quit and decline “Restore pages” if asked.

Do not call the number

Real Apple support does not lock your browser. See fake Apple support call popup.
