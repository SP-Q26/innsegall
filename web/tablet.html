Bad link on this iPad or tablet · walk through it here

Breathe. Couch panic is real · most scares end with a closed tab, not a ruined device. Innsegall does not scan your iPad · we say that plainly. A scout on a Mac only lights up that computer · it cannot prove this tablet is clear.

Work through the steps below on this tablet first. When the worry is the Mac, we light that path next.

Step 1 · First minute (stay calm)

  1. Stop tapping · close the tab.
  2. If the page is stuck or screaming, force-quit Safari and reopen without restoring tabs.
  3. Do not install anything the page offers · do not add a configuration profile you did not plan.
  4. Do not type Apple ID · email · or bank passwords on that site.

Step 2 · Match what happened

Pick the row that fits · do only that row’s next move.

  • I only read the page · you are usually fine. Optional: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → remove that site.
  • Something downloaded · Files → Downloads → delete it if you did not open it · do not open unknown files.
  • A profile was offered or installed · Settings → General → VPN & Device Management · remove profiles you do not recognize (keep work or school profiles you expect).
  • Safari feels hijacked · review Safari extensions · clear website data for suspicious sites.

Step 3 · If you typed a password

That is the horn · not shame. On another device you trust: change email password first · then banking · sign out other sessions. Account care comes before any Mac app.
