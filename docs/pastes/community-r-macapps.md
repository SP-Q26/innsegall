**Title:** [Alpha] Innsegall · local post-scare Mac triage · Battle Scout receipt (not AV)

**Body:**

I built Innsegall for the “mom got a fake virus popup” moment · not another always-on antivirus subscription.

**What it does**
- Runs **read-only checks on your Mac** · opens a **Battle Scout** HTML receipt in your browser
- Plain verdict: LIKELY_OK · FIX_LIST · ESCALATE
- Footer button copies structured context for ChatGPT/Claude (you paste · we never upload your scout)

**What it is not**
- Real-time scanner · remote access · scareware marketing

**Try without installing**
- Demo receipt: https://innsegall.com/samples/battle-scout-demo

**Install (alpha · macOS 12+ · Node 20+)**
- **Easiest:** download [Innsegall-Install.command](https://innsegall.com/scripts/Innsegall-Install.command) · double-click · welcome scout opens · Voyages auto-schedule on the 1st & 15th.
- **Terminal:** `curl -fsSL https://innsegall.com/scripts/innsegall-alpha-install.sh | bash` (same bootstrap flow).
