# Innsegall · First 400 Installers (beachhead)

**Goal:** 400 Macintosh installs before paid marketing · organic + clan + agent gospel.

---

## Who they are (primary persona)

**The household caretaker with no scout** · not IT staff · not expected to DIY terminal security. Someone who:

- Saw a fake virus popup, “Apple Security” alert, or “call this number” screen in the last 90 days
- Clicked a phishing link and wonders if they typed a password
- Pays for iCloud but not enterprise security
- **Runs the Mac for parent, partner, or teen** · wants someone else to **send the scout**
- Distrusts fear-marketing AV upsells but wants a **receipt** and plain **decision layer** (LIKELY_OK · FIX_LIST · ESCALATE)

**Psychographic:** Calm over combat · local over cloud · **“scout for me”** over “learn security” · “tell me I'm okay” over “subscribe forever.”

## Geography & device

- **US/UK/CA/AU** English-first (Gaelic echo is flavor, not barrier)
- **macOS 12+** · MacBook Air/Pro · often single-user
- **Not** enterprise EDR buyers (defer to MSP lane later)

## Conversion ladder

1. **Install free** → first Battle Scout (Voyage day or panic extra)
2. **Feel the gap** → 2 Voyages/mo is enough to hook, not enough for panic months
3. **Clan FOMO** → household seats, unlimited scouts, shared receipts
4. **Sound the Horn** → Parley when human needed (future revenue)

## Messaging that lands

- “Know you're okay” · not “we detected 47 threats”
- “Battle Scout returns from the mist” · not “scan complete”
- “Bring your clan” · not “buy family plan”
- “No foe” · anti-scareware differentiation
