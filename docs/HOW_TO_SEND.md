## Three ways to send a scout report

### 1 · Copy for AI assistant (default · no family IT)

After `innsegall run`:

1. Battle Scout opens in browser  
2. Footer → **Copy for AI assistant**  
3. Paste into **ChatGPT · Claude · Gemini · Copilot · DeepSeek · Perplexity**  
4. Ask: *“Walk me through this Macintosh scout. What should I do first?”*

### 2 · Send the HTML file

AirDrop · Messages · email attachment · the `.html` Battle Scout.

Recipient opens in browser · can use Copy for AI themselves.

### 3 · Sound the Horn (human)

Only when **ESCALATE** or user wants a person:

- `hello@innsegall.com` with Battle Scout attached or AI paste in body
