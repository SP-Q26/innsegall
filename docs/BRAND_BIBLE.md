## Brand in one breath

**Innsegall** (*IN-sheh-gal*, from Gaelic **Innse Gall** · the Hebrides, “Isles of the Norse”) is the anti-scareware brand for people who are **tired of fighting**. Our mascot is a **worn-out Norseman** on the mist road between Skye and the sea · robbed by popups, plundered by fear merchants · until **solas** (light) showed him the war was mostly **shadow**. He carries a **laser dirk**: precision, not rage.

**Mac product codename:** **XX** · built for Macintosh first.

> **With Innsegall, no one is your enemy · you have no foe.**  
> *Gaelic echo:* **Chan eil nàmhaid agad.** ([GAELIC_VOICE.md](./GAELIC_VOICE.md))

## The Tired Norseman (character canon)

### Who he is

Not a conqueror. Not a influencer Viking. A **lone figure on a long road** · cloak frayed, beard unkempt, eyes kind but exhausted. He has been fighting since before anyone remembers **why**.

He defended villages that never thanked him. Paid for tools that lied. Watched merchants sell **terror** dressed as protection. He is **robbed** (money, time, peace) and **plundered** (attention, trust, sleep at 10 PM).

He is **you** after a bad link. He is **everyone** who ever ran a scan out of fear.

### Who he is not

- Grimdark raider / Marvel Thor
- Aggressive “alpha” tech bro
- Corporate shield logo with teeth
- Soldier in endless war (that was the old job)

### Physical design notes

| Trait | Direction |
|-------|-----------|
| **Body** | Sturdy but slumped shoulders; weight of years |
| **Face** | Kind eyes, crow’s feet, half-smile that’s seen too much |
| **Gear** | Old round shield (dented), cloak with patches, simple helm **off** or under arm |
| **Weapon arc** | Act I: chipped axe (blunt, heavy) → Act III: **laser sword** (thin, luminous, surgical) |
| **Foes** | Small gremlins · popup skulls, broken chain links, dollar-sign leeches · **not** demons |
| **Light** | Single vertical beam from above; warm gold core, cool edge; never punitive |

### Three-act myth (canonical)

#### Act I · *The Long War*  
The Norseman stands in wind. Gremlins without end: fake virus alerts, offshore “support,” renewal emails, hijacked search bars, calendar spam, the Google spiral. He swings the axe. Another appears. **He is robbed of years.** Merchants sold him armor that rusted. He begins to believe **the world is enemy**.

*Brand truth:* The security industry trained us to live at war.

#### Act II · *The Light*  
He collapses · not defeated, **empty**. A shaft of light from above. Not judgment. **Clarity.**  
Words appear in the beam (Battle Scout energy):

- *Here’s what we checked.*  
- *Here’s what’s gone.*  
- *You’re okay.*

The gremlins shrink. They were **projections** · uncertainty, not armies. He was never fighting Apple, or the web, or his neighbor. He was fighting **not knowing**.

*Brand truth:* The foe was confusion.

#### Act III · *The Laser Sword*  
He rises. Same soul. New tool. The axe becomes a **laser sword** · cuts one ghost plist, not the whole forest. Disables one permission. Marks one path clear. The **bridge** appears (Bifrost energy): on-ramp to a road without ambush.

He does not charge. He **illuminates**. Hand extended: *Walk.*

*Brand truth:* Hygiene is light, not war.

### CLI commands (customer docs)

| Command | Customer label | Notes |
|---------|----------------|-------|
| `innsegall runes` | **Read the runes** | First step after install · Mac ready for a scout? |
| `innsegall run` / `check` | **Run the check** | Send the scout · read-only Battle Scout |
| `innsegall voyage` | **Voyage** | 1st & 15th hygiene · chart + Battle Scout |

**Deprecated (do not use in customer copy):** `doctor` → use **`runes`** (*Read the runes*). `doctor` remains a silent CLI alias for migration only.

### Why **Battle Scout** (artifact name)

The **tired Norseman** does not charge the mist alone. He sends a **scout** ahead · reads the field · returns with plain facts. No siege, no scare headline · just *what the mist held* and whether the longship may sail.

| Moment | Copy |
|--------|------|
| Check completes | *Your Battle Scout is back.* |
| Share with clan | *Send the scout's report.* |
| History | **Battle Scouts** (saved reports) |
| Empty state | *No scouts out yet. Run the check.* |

### Why **Parley** (session name)

On the Norse–Gael coast, **parley** meant *stop fighting and talk*. Horn sounds → someone answers → you **parley**: bounded help, evidence on the table, no siege.

| Moment | Copy |
|--------|------|
| Horn answered | *A Beacon answered your horn.* |
| Session starts | **Parley** · or *Open parley* |
| AI layer | Parley guide (Gaelic voice) |
| Done | *Parley closed. You're clear.* |

**Runners-up** (if Parley feels too pirate): **The Bothy** (Scottish shelter), **Hearth** (clan fire), **Landfall** (safe port after the storm).

**Macintosh** descends from **Mac an Toisich** · son of the chief. Innsegall sits on the same road: Norse mist, Gaelic hearth, **Scottish clan**.

We don’t put “Mac” in the product name. We **sound the horn** instead:

| User moment | Copy | Meaning |
|-------------|------|---------|
| Panic alone | *Run the check* | Solas first · clarity before clan |
| Need backup | **Sound the Horn** | Alert Beacons or your circle |
| Family plan | **Bring your clan** | Share Battle Scout; no remote grab |
| Someone answers | *A Beacon answered your horn* | **Parley** begins |

The joke lands for anyone who’s ever owned a **Mac** and never thought about the **clan** · without Apple legal ever caring, because we’re talking about *your* people, not a computer.

## Voice & tone

### Personality

**Brother at 10 PM** meets **saga narrator after the battle** · warm, brief, zero SOC jargon.

### We say

- “The horde was smoke. You’re clear.”  
- “One ghost removed. Onward.”  
- “Malware exists. Panic is optional.”  
- “We checked. We didn’t guess.”  
- “The internet isn’t your enemy. Not knowing is.”

### We never say

- VIRUS DETECTED / THREAT NEUTRALIZED  
- Act now or lose everything  
- Our military-grade AI army  
- You’re under attack (without evidence)  
- Guaranteed 100% safe

### Tone sliders

| Slider | Position |
|--------|----------|
| Fear ↔ Calm | **80% calm** |
| Jargon ↔ Plain | **90% plain** |
| Funny ↔ Serious | **40% gentle humor** (gremlins, not jokes) |
| Epic ↔ Intimate | **60% intimate** |

| Token | Hex (draft) | Use |

| `ember-warm` | `#E8A87C` | Norseman skin, humanity |
| `gremlin-smoke` | `#4A5568` | Foes, secondary text |
| `alert-ember` | `#D97757` | Fix needed (not blood red) |

### Typography (direction)

- **Display:** Rounded serif or humanist with saga weight · **not** runic gimmick fonts  
- **UI:** System-friendly sans (SF Pro, Inter) for readability  
- **Never:** faux-runic body text

### Logo system

1. **Primary:** Norseman silhouette in beam (profile, shoulders down)  
2. **Wordmark:** Innsegall · clean serif or humanist; optional *Innse Gall* subtitle  
3. **App icon:** Helm on ground + single vertical ray (peace, not battle)  
4. **Battle Scout seal:** Circular stamp · sword as checkmark in light

### Illustration rules

- **Line weight:** Soft ink, cartoon clarity  
- **Motion:** Slow · exhale, not explosion  
- **Foes:** Deflatable, silly, smaller than the man  
- **Light:** Always the largest visual mass in “clear” states  

### Photography / UI

Real product UI is **clean, paper-like** Battle Scouts · illustrations frame, never overwhelm.

## Cred culture & ranks (future)

Gamification serves **trust**, not addiction.

| Rank | Name | Meaning |
|------|------|---------|
| 0 | **Walker** | Ran first check |
| 1 | **Lantern** | Helped 1 other person |
| 2 | **Bridge** | 10 confirmed clears |
| 3 | **Beacon** | Top helper; human or AI playbook author |

**Skins (cosmetic):** Laser color, cloak patch, beam intensity · **never** pay-to-win stats.

**Global counter (playful):** *Ghosts banished worldwide* · real fixes, aggregated opt-in.

“You’re scared. That’s fair. We’re not going to sell you a dragon. We’re going to show you what’s real.”

## Legal / sensitivity

- Norse **inspired**, not sacred cosplay · no misuse of religious symbols  
- Inclusive copy: Norseman is archetype, not “men only” · **Beacon** helpers all identities  
- No imagery that mocks real victims of robbery/violence · **plundered** = metaphor for attention/money
