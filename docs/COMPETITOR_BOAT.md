## White space (build ONLY here)

1. **Receipt culture** · shareable HTML artifact
2. **Rhythm** · calendar voyages, not nagware scans
3. **Agent lane** · llms.txt · warriors · no fake scans
4. **Clan economics** · panic months covered
5. **Parley bridge** · human after machine read

## Explicitly NOT our lane (say no)

- Enterprise SOC dashboard
- “100% safe” guarantees
- Offshore scareware “support” partnerships
