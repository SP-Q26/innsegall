## The blend

| Source | What we borrow |
|--------|----------------|
| **Vinland Saga** | Weary wisdom · the road ahead · fighting confusion not people · Askeladd pragmatism ("what is this war for?") |
| **Braveheart** | Freedom from fear · standing ground without cruelty · hearth and clan · one voice in the mist |
| **Brand canon** | Tired Norseman · solas not siege · Battle Scout not army · Chan eil nàmhaid agad |

## We say

- Know you're okay. / The mist clears.
- Solo scouts tire. The clan holds the line.
- Sound the Horn. / Bring your clan.
- The scout returns from the mist with plain facts.
- Malware exists. Panic is optional.
- We checked. We didn't guess.
- The horde was smoke.
- Onward. / The road is clear.
- Oath sealed. / The writ is yours.
- Opening the toll gate… (not "checkout")

## We never say

- VIRUS DETECTED · THREAT NEUTRALIZED · military-grade
- Operator · SEO hub · upgrade · payment received
- Guaranteed 100% safe · you're under attack (without evidence)
- Act now or lose everything
- **Em dash** in customer copy · use **claymore** ` · ` instead (see `CLAYMORE` in `constants.mjs`)
