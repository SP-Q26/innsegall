## What we own

| Moment | Product behavior |
|--------|------------------|
| Fake virus popup · bad link · password scare | Read-only scout · Battle Scout HTML · **You're clear** / **Small fixes** / **Get help** |
| Need proof for family, IT, or insurance | Shareable report · stays local until you export |
| AI assistant spiral without evidence | **Copy for AI** · `innsegall-battle-scout-ai/v1` · honor triage_level |
| Quarterly hygiene without AV bill | **Voyage** on 1st & 15th · chart over time |
| iPhone/iPad panic (wrong device) | Honest guides · Mac scout when the computer matters |

## What we do not own

- Enterprise EDR, SOC, 24/7 monitoring
- Windows/Linux-only endpoints (Macintosh alpha first)
- Guaranteed “100% clean” or remote access without visible user consent
- Persistence blocking or outbound firewall (complements: KnockKnock, BlockBlock, LuLu **after** scout)
